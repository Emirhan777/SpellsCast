SpellsCast includes an offline iPhone game, rune practice, and a controller for its companion browser game. No account, purchase, subscription, or login is required.

VERSION 1.2.3
This release refreshes the store screenshots and gameplay preview. The preview mainly shows real on-device Finger mode, followed by the native controller in an online two-player duel. Offline on-device play remains solo. Tilt mode uses the iPhone's motion sensors. The controller disables iOS swipe-back navigation so sideways rune strokes do not dismiss a room opened from an invitation. Use Leave room to exit.

INVITE A SECOND PLAYER
1. Open https://emirhan777.github.io/SpellsCast/ on a separate computer and keep it open. Click the page once to enable its audio.
2. On the iPhone, choose Join an existing game. Scan the computer's QR (allow camera access) or enter its six-digit room code.
3. Before starting a round, tap Invite second player on the connected screen or wand controls. The native share sheet contains a link to this same room. The computer switches to Wizard duel.
4. Send the invite to a second phone, or copy it. The recipient can use the browser controller or choose Open in SpellsCast to use the installed app. The first phone stays connected during sharing; actual network loss still requires reconnecting.
5. With both phones connected, select Start duel. The first correct cast received by the host claims each target for one point. Both phones display their individual score, opponent score, timer, and result. Highest score wins. Select Rematch to play again.
6. Full rooms disable inviting; no third player can join. Invitations are hidden during active rounds. If system sharing is unavailable, a dialog provides Copy invite link and a selectable URL. Offline on-device play remains solo.

CASTING IN CONNECTED PLAY
Choose Use motion wand (allow motion access) or Use touch controls. Point at the computer screen. Hold the cast pad, draw a rune by moving the phone, then release. In touch mode, draw on the pad and lift your finger. Classic mode supports one phone. Start a round using Start game or a recognized spell.

ON-DEVICE GAME: NO SECOND SCREEN NEEDED
1. Choose Play on this device. The game opens in landscape with Tilt selected.
2. Hold anywhere, allow motion if prompted, tilt through a displayed rune, then release. A recognized spell starts a round; a tap or permission approval alone does not.
3. To test without sensors, select Finger in the top menu. Draw the red V-shaped Expel rune directly on the screen and lift. Continue casting runes matching the targets. Cast again after game over to restart.
4. Tilt and Finger keep separate local best scores. Switching modes confirms ending the current run. Mirror explains AirPlay and HDMI setup; start actual Screen Mirroring in iOS Control Center. The full landscape game is mirrored. Local play pauses while backgrounded or while the mirroring guide is open.

START A SCREEN FROM THE APP
Choose Start a game, then Create a game link. Send or copy it to a computer. Open it there and select Open game on this screen. Return to the app; it pairs automatically. An unused screen-launch link expires after ten minutes.

OFFLINE PRACTICE
Choose Practice your spells. Draw the displayed rune with a finger; tap another spell to change the guide. No camera, sensors, network, or second device is needed.

The camera only scans room QR codes; images are not saved or uploaded. Motion becomes wand coordinates. Online play requires a computer or compatible TV browser showing the companion game; the phone is its controller. On-device play works offline and is mirrored through iOS Screen Mirroring or compatible HDMI hardware.
